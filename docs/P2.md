## 5. The video

**Length:** 3 to 4 minutes (check the assignment for a limit). **Format:** 1080p, landscape or portrait (pick one and keep it; portrait suits iPhone screen recordings).

### 5.1 Structure and script outline

| # | Part | Length | On screen | Narration (who) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Title card | 5 s | "Geometric ARt - Checkpoint 2" + four names | none |
| 1 | Intro | 20 s | Sketch 1 from the PDF, then the running app beside it | A: "Geometric ARt lets you build geometric patterns on a board anchored in AR. Here's sketch one from our proposal and the app running on an iPhone." |
| 2 | **Title: "[A's name] - AR board"** | 40 s | Second-phone shot of A scanning the table, then screen recording: reticle, tap to place, board with dashed dividers, walk around it, golden star on the board | A: plane detection, placement, anchoring, rendering the star, the build pipeline |
| 3 | **Title: "[C's name] - Interface"** | 40 s | Home screen; split-screen with sketch 1; open Tools, split-screen with sketch 2; layer widget, "2x2" pill, greyed-out Hand toggle | C: how each sketched element maps to the screen; why Hand is disabled (screen-only for the MVP) |
| 4 | **Title: "[B's name] - Drawing engine"** | 45 s | Place snap points, draw a line between them, draw a circle, Undo, Redo | B: snapping, the command stack behind undo/redo, the tests that check it |
| 5 | **Title: "[D's name] - Input and tools"** | 40 s | Touch drawing, Stamps (and any other tool that works), optional lesson step 1 | D: touch-to-board input, the tools, how QA checks each build |
| 6 | Reflection | 30 s | Short clips or a slide with three bullets | Each person one sentence, or one person for all: what works, what was hard (e.g. iOS signing, integration), what's next (lesson, tiling, save/load for checkpoint 3) |
| 7 | End card | 5 s | Repo link + names | none |

Narration and footage order can differ from the build order; use the order above because it follows the user's path through the app.

### 5.2 Shot list (Saturday 7-10 pm)

Film in a bright room on a plain, textured table (wood, a placemat, or paper with print). AR plane detection is poor on glossy or white surfaces.

| Shot | How | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| S1 Over-the-shoulder | Second phone films the iPhone from behind, 10-15 s: scan, tap to place, walk around. **This is the proof it runs on a phone** | A |
| S2 Placement | iPhone screen recording: scan, reticle, place, walk around | A |
| S3 Home screen | Screen recording: hold still on the home screen 3 s, then tap each element | C |
| S4 Tools panel | Open Tools, tap a few cells, close | C |
| S5 Drawing | Snap points, line, circle, undo, redo, slowly | B |
| S6 Tools / touch | Stamps and other working tools, touch drawing | D |
| S7 Sketches | Screenshots of PDF pages 3 and 4 for the split-screens | D |

**Screen recording on iPhone:** Settings > Control Center > add Screen Recording; swipe down from the top-right corner, tap record, wait for the 3 s countdown. Do not record narration through the phone mic while filming; narration is done separately (5.4). Record each shot 2-3 times and keep the best.

Airdrop or upload all clips to a shared folder (Google Drive / OneDrive) named `P2_footage/` with file names `S2_A_take2.mov`.

### 5.3 Editing (D)

- Tool: **iMovie** (Mac/iPhone, free) or **CapCut** (free, any OS). Use whichever D knows.
- Title cards: plain background, white text, name + part, 2-3 s each.
- Split-screens for part 3: sketch left, app right (iMovie: "Side by Side" overlay; CapCut: picture-in-picture).
- Speed up slow parts (scanning) to 2x; never speed up drawing.
- Captions on any clip not recorded on the phone (section 3).
- Export 1080p MP4. Upload as the assignment asks (file upload, or an unlisted YouTube link if a file is too large).

### 5.4 Narration (each person, Sunday by 2 pm)

- Watch your section's footage, write 4-6 sentences, and time it to your segment length.
- Record in a quiet room with **Voice Memos** on your phone, held 15-20 cm from your mouth. Send the file to D named `N_<letter>_<name>.m4a`.
- Start with your name: "I'm [name], and I built ...". This satisfies the "each member narrates" requirement even without title cards; we do both.
- Say what you built and one technical detail, not just what's on screen.
